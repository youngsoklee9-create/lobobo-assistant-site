Privacy Policy — Lobobo Personal Assistant

Effective date: 2026-10-06

Who uses this app

Lobobo is a personal-use application. Its only user is the owner of the Google account that authorizes it. It is not distributed to the public.

Google data accessed

  • Google Calendar: read, create, update and delete events on the owner's calendars.
  • Gmail (read-only): messages under a single label designated by the owner.
  • Google Drive (read-only): files in folders designated by the owner.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the owner's own assistant features (schedule lookup, task and call/message summaries). Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties, except that the owner's own AI model provider processes requests made by the owner, and summaries are saved to the owner's own note workspace.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete stored tokens and data from their own computer.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
